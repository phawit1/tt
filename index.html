QUICK GUIDE

How to play

  1. Set up your space. Place your iPad or laptop steadily and make sure your body is visible.
  2. Enable the camera. Stand back far enough for the camera to see your body.
  3. Choose a mission. Follow the coaching message and complete the movement.
  4. Earn points. Hold the balance target or complete a full movement cycle.

Student project prototype, not a medical or professional fitness assessment. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness, or discomfort.
